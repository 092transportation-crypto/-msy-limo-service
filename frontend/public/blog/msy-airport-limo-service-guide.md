# MSY Airport Limo Service Guide: Everything You Need to Know

Louis Armstrong New Orleans International Airport (MSY) sits in Kenner, about 14 miles west of the French Quarter, and welcomes travelers arriving for everything from a weekend of Bourbon Street to a week-long convention at the Morial Center. A limo service — really, a chauffeured car service that includes sedans, SUVs, Sprinter vans, and true stretch limousines — is the difference between an arrival that starts with a plan and one that starts with a scramble. This guide covers how MSY limo service actually works, from booking to drop-off.

## What "Limo Service" Means at MSY

Most travelers picturing "a limo" imagine the stretch version reserved for weddings and prom night. In practice, an airport limo service is a broader category: any pre-reserved, chauffeur-driven vehicle booked in advance for a flat rate, as opposed to a taxi you flag or a rideshare you hail on demand. At MSY, that includes executive sedans for one to three travelers, Cadillac Escalade SUVs for four to six, Mercedes Sprinter vans for larger groups, and stretch limousines for events that call for a grand arrival. All of them share the same operating model: booked ahead, flight-tracked, and priced before you ever get in the car.

## How Booking Works

Reserving a car for MSY takes about a minute online, or a short call to dispatch. You'll need three pieces of information: your flight number, your drop-off address, and your party size with a rough luggage count. The flight number is the most important — it's what allows dispatch to track your arrival automatically, so the vehicle is dispatched against your actual landing time rather than the printed schedule. You'll get a written confirmation with a flat rate attached, and that number does not change between booking and drop-off.

Book as early as you can, particularly around Mardi Gras, Jazz Fest, French Quarter Fest, Essence Fest, and any week the Morial Convention Center has a major show — vehicle classes sell out during those weeks well before the date itself. A standard weekday transfer, by contrast, can usually be booked a day or two ahead with no trouble.

## What Happens the Day You Fly

Once your reservation is confirmed, there is nothing left for you to do. Dispatch begins tracking your flight from departure, and if it runs early or late, your pickup shifts automatically — no phone call, no app notification, no fee for the adjustment. At MSY, every airport pickup also includes 45 minutes of complimentary wait time on domestic arrivals and 60 minutes on international arrivals, timed from actual touchdown rather than the scheduled time, which comfortably covers a slow customs line or a bag that takes its time on the carousel.

## Curbside vs. Meet-and-Greet

You choose your pickup style when you book. With standard curbside service, included on every transfer, your chauffeur contacts you as you land and pulls to the arrivals curb outside baggage claim — you walk out one door and get in. With meet-and-greet, a modest add-on, your chauffeur waits inside the terminal at the bottom of the baggage-claim escalators holding a sign with your name, and walks your bags to the vehicle with you. First-time visitors, families managing kids and a luggage cart, and arriving clients on a [corporate car service](https://msylimoservice.com/services/corporate-transportation) booking tend to get the most out of the inside greeting.

## Choosing the Right Vehicle

- **Executive sedan:** one to three passengers with standard luggage — the default for solo business travel and couples.

- **Luxury SUV (Cadillac Escalade):** four to six passengers, or anyone with extra bags, golf clubs, or festival gear.

- **Sprinter van:** seven to thirteen passengers, ideal for wedding parties, sports teams, and convention groups moving together.

- **Stretch limousine:** reserved less for a plain airport run and more for the occasion itself — anniversaries, milestone birthdays, or an arrival meant to be memorable.

Since rates are quoted per vehicle rather than per passenger, groups routinely find that splitting an SUV or Sprinter costs less per seat than several separate rideshare fares, in a nicer vehicle, with a single guaranteed pickup.

## The Ride Into the City

The standard route from MSY into New Orleans is I-10 East, and most trips to downtown or the French Quarter take 20 to 30 minutes in normal traffic — expect up to 45 minutes at rush hour or after a Saints game lets out at the Caesars Superdome. Because the rate is flat, that traffic is entirely your chauffeur's problem, not a line item on your bill. Chauffeurs who run this corridor daily also know the detours: Airline Highway when I-10 backs up, and the right approach into the French Quarter's one-way grid when a festival closes a block without warning.

## What a Legitimate Flat Rate Includes

A proper flat rate at MSY covers the vehicle and chauffeur, real-time flight tracking, the complimentary wait-time window, luggage handling from the carousel to the vehicle, and bottled water in a detailed, late-model car. Add-ons like meet-and-greet, child car seats, or an extra stop are itemized separately and confirmed before you book — never sprung on you at drop-off. If a quote excludes airport fees or charges "wait time from scheduled arrival" rather than actual touchdown, you're not looking at a real flat rate.

## Special Situations Worth Planning For

Cruise passengers flying into MSY before boarding at the Port of New Orleans should book the transfer alongside their flights — the port sits about 20 to 30 minutes from the airport, and a reserved car removes the one variable that can ruin an embarkation morning. Groups of five or more should default to a Sprinter van rather than splitting across cars. And anyone with mobility needs should note it at booking so the right vehicle and a little extra time are built in from the start; door-to-door beats every shared alternative on that front.

## Navigating the MSY Terminal for First-Timers

Louis Armstrong International's terminal is compact and easy to learn on the first visit: gates feed into a central concourse, and escalators bring arriving passengers down to a single baggage-claim level. Ground transportation, including both curbside pickup and meet-and-greet staging, happens on that same lower level, which is part of why the terminal rarely feels confusing even to someone who's never flown into New Orleans before. If you've booked meet-and-greet, look for your chauffeur near the bottom of the escalators rather than out at the curb — that's the one detail that trips up a small number of first-time flyers who default to walking straight outside out of habit from other airports.

## Common Mistakes First-Time Bookers Make

- **Booking a sedan for a group of five:** comfortable capacity matters more than squeezing everyone in — an SUV or Sprinter is the right call once you pass three passengers with luggage.

- **Giving an estimated arrival time instead of a flight number:** an estimate can't be tracked automatically the way a flight number can, which is the entire mechanism that protects you from a delay.

- **Assuming festival-week pricing is different:** it isn't — flat rates hold during Mardi Gras and Jazz Fest exactly as they do on a quiet Tuesday, but vehicle classes do sell out, so the mistake is waiting too long to book, not worrying about price.

- **Skipping the return-trip booking:** leaving the departure transfer for "later" is the single most common thing travelers wish they'd handled at the same time as the arrival.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**What's the difference between a limo service and a taxi at MSY?**

A limo/car service is reserved in advance for a flat rate confirmed at booking, with flight tracking and a guaranteed pickup. A taxi is hailed at the curb for a regulated meter or flat fare with no advance reservation.

**How far in advance should I book MSY limo service?**

A day or two ahead is fine for a standard weekday trip. Book as soon as your flights are confirmed for Mardi Gras, Jazz Fest, Essence Fest, or any week the Morial Convention Center has a major event.

**Does the flat rate change if my flight is delayed?**

No. Flight tracking and the standard complimentary wait-time window are included in every airport fare, and the rate is confirmed at booking regardless of delays.

**Can I book both my arrival and departure transfer together?**

Yes, and we recommend it — booking both legs at once locks in your departure pickup, which is especially useful for early-morning flights out of MSY.
